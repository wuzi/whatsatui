# Animated stickers and received videos

Animated WhatsApp WebP stickers play in the conversation and sticker picker, including locally sent stickers. Advance frames in the app: Ghostty supports Kitty images but does not yet implement native animation frames. Retain static image/avatar behavior. Decode away from the UI, cancel hidden jobs, preserve timing, and release every terminal image when hidden, resized or stopped. Bound retained animation frames to 96, 160×160 thumbnails, source canvas to 1 megapixel, duration to 30 seconds and source frames to 1,500 and total decoded source pixels to 64 million. Sample fast/long animations at at most 20 fps; oversized or malformed animations fall back to the first frame. Existing 16 MiB preview and eight-visible-preview limits remain.

Received non-view-once videos retain downloadable references and captions. Clicking the video or using the existing play key opens an mpv window with sound, seeking and normal video quality. Reuse the verified 50 MiB cache and private playback snapshot. One player owns audio or video at a time; existing pause, speed, stop and progress controls remain available. Native window pause/close must agree with TUI state. Closing a video is normal completion. Expiry, deletion, account changes and quitting still stop playback and release the child/snapshot. Never automatically play or download videos. Old `[video]` placeholders have no download references; only newly received/replayed records can become playable.

One Cargo process; shared root target; `-j 2`, `--test-threads=2`. Synthetic/offline verification and null outputs for native player tests; no live account or desktop clipboard. No new Rust dependencies. Inline implementation, one final independent review, local merge/release, preserve evidence, no push.
